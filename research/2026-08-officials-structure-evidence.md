## TOPIC A — Structure of a Philippine Municipal Government

Governing statute: Republic Act No. 7160, "Local Government Code of 1991" (enacted October 10, 1991; effective January 1, 1992 per Sec. 536). Full text: LawPhil, https://lawphil.net/statutes/repacts/ra1991/ra_7160_1991.html (retrieved 2026-08-26; page is very large and was only partially readable in this session — see per-item notes). Mirrors used for verification: ChanRobles Book III (https://www.chanrobles.com/localgov3.htm), Supreme Court E-Library decisions quoting the Code verbatim, DILG-hosted materials, and official LGU websites quoting Code text.

### A1. The Executive — Municipal Mayor

**Citation:** RA 7160, Book III, Title II (The Municipality), Chapter 3, Article I, **Sec. 444** — "The Chief Executive: Powers, Duties, Functions, and Compensation."
- Verified: Wikipedia "Local chief executives" states "Municipal Mayor, Book III, Title II, Chapter 3, Article I, Section 444" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_chief_executives, retrieved 2026-08-26).
- Verified: DILG Region V "Municipal Mayors Reference Guide (7th ed., 2022)" cites "[Section 444(b)(1)(ii), RA No. …]" (https://region5.dilg.gov.ph/lgrrc/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/2022-Checklist-Municipal-Mayor-rev2.pdf, surfaced via search, retrieved 2026-08-26).
- Verified: NDV Law firm explainer quotes Sec. 444(a): "The municipal mayor, as the chief executive of the municipal government, shall exercise such powers and performs such duties and functions as provided by this Code and other laws…" (https://ndvlaw.com/what-are-the-powers-and-functions-of-a-mayor-in-the-philippines/, retrieved 2026-08-26).

**Term of office:** Three (3) years; maximum of three (3) consecutive terms — **Sec. 43(b)**, Book I (echoing Art. X, Sec. 8 of the 1987 Constitution).
- Verified: Supreme Court, *Aldovino, Jr. v. COMELEC*, G.R. No. 184836: "Section 43(b) reads: Term of Office. — x x x (b) No local elective official shall serve for more than three (3) consecutive terms in the same position." (https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/53222, surfaced via search, retrieved 2026-08-26.)

**Core powers/responsibilities (brief list, summarized from Sec. 444 as quoted in the sources above):**
1. Exercises general executive powers and control over all programs/projects of the municipal government;
2. Ensures delivery of basic services and adequate facilities;
3. Enforces all laws and ordinances within the municipality;
4. Initiates/proposes legislative measures to the Sangguniang Bayan and vetoes ordinances (item veto allowed for fiscal measures);
5. Appoints officials and employees subject to civil service law (appointment of officers already covered by other appointment rules is treated separately — see caveat below);
6. Prepares the executive/local development agenda and budget proposals;
7. Represents the municipality in transactions and exercises such other powers as provided by the Code and other laws.

⚠️ **Caveat:** This is a faithful summary, not verbatim text; the full enumerated list in Sec. 444(b)(1)–(8) should be quoted verbatim from LawPhil before any production publication.

### A2. The Vice Mayor

**Role — presiding officer of the Sangguniang Bayan:** **Sec. 446(a)** describes the Sangguniang Bayan as "composed of the municipal vice-mayor **as the presiding officer**, the regular sanggunian members, the president of the municipal chapter of the liga ng mga barangay, the president of the pambayang pederasyon ng mga sangguniang kabataan, and the sectoral representatives, as members."
- Verified verbatim via multiple independent quotations: Supreme Court E-Library / Lawyerly, *La Carlota City v. Rojo*, G.R. No. 181367 quoting "SECTION 446. Composition. (a)…" (https://lawyerly.ph/juris/view/cd611 and https://nlpdl.nlp.gov.ph/SC01/2012apr/181367_delcastillo.htm); Municipality of Quezon, Quirino official site: "Under Section 446 of RA 7160. Composition…" (https://quezonquezon.gov.ph/home/documents/, dated 2025-09-18 in search index, retrieved 2026-08-26); Santa Fe (Nueva Vizcaya) RA 7160 PDF (https://santaelena-cn.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/RA-7160.pdf).
- Note: Sec. 447 then confers the Sangguniang Bayan's powers, duties, and functions (confirmed by LGU pages, e.g., Municipality of Bay: "Section 447 … provide for the powers, duties, and functions of the Sangguniang Bayan", https://bay.gov.ph/sangguniang-bayan). ⚠️ Do not confuse 446 (composition) with 447 (powers).

**Succession:**
- If the mayor's office becomes permanently vacant, the **vice mayor becomes mayor**; if the vice mayoralty becomes permanently vacant, the highest-ranking (then next-highest-ranking) sanggunian member becomes vice mayor — **Book I, Sec. 44**.
  - Verified: Supreme Court, *Gamboa, Jr. v. Aguirre, Jr.*, G.R. No. 134213 (https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/37313): "If a permanent vacancy occurs in the offices of the governor, vice-governor, mayor or vice-mayor, the highest ranking Sanggunian member … shall become the governor, vice-governor, mayor or vice-mayor, as the case may be…"
- During temporary vacancy/incapacity of the mayor, the vice mayor exercises mayoral powers (Book I, Sec. 46, as referenced in LGU function statements, e.g., Kasibu official site quoting vice-mayor duties: "Assume the office of the Municipal mayor for the unexpired term … in the event of permanent vacancy as provided for in Section 44, Book I … Exercise the powers … of the Municipal Mayor in cases of temporary vacancy as provided for in Section 46, Book I" — https://kasibunv.gov.ph/sangguniang-bayan, snippet via search, retrieved 2026-08-26).
- Other standard vice-mayor functions (quoted consistently across LGU sites, e.g., Maria Aurora, Kapalong, La Trinidad): preside over SB sessions; sign warrants drawn on the municipal treasury for SB expenditures; appoint SB employees subject to civil service law.
- ⚠️ **Weakness:** The standalone Code section number for the municipal vice mayor's powers/duties (commonly cited as Sec. 448) was **not directly verified against primary text in this session**. Verify on LawPhil before publishing the number. (For cities, the parallel composition provision is Sec. 457 and city vice-mayor provisions follow it — see NLP-hosted *La Carlota* decision quoting Sec. 457.)

### A3. Sangguniang Bayan — Composition

**Exact provision: Sec. 446(a)** (verbatim quotation above). Members:
1. **Municipal vice mayor** — presiding officer (no vote except to break a tie; practice consistent with Wikipedia "Sangguniang Bayan" article, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Bayan, retrieved 2026-08-26);
2. **Eight (8) elected regular sanggunian members** (councilors) elected at-large. Note: Sec. 446(a)'s phrase is "the regular sanggunian members"; the count of eight is fixed by election practice under the Code and reflected in the *La Carlota* decision summary: "the Sangguniang Bayan is composed of eight (8) regular members, the Liga ng mga Barangay President, the SK Federation President, the Vice-Mayor as Presiding Officer…" (SC E-Library G.R. 181367, https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/1/54801). Exception: Pateros (12 councilors, two districts, per RA 7887 — cited in the Wikipedia article).
3. **Ex-officio members:**
   - President of the **municipal chapter of the Liga ng mga Barangay** (ABC president);
   - President of the **pambayang pederasyon ng mga sangguniang kabataan** (SK municipal federation president);
   - **Indigenous Peoples Mandatory Representative (IPMR)** where applicable — ⚠️ **not part of Sec. 446's text as quoted above**; its basis is separate (RA 8371 / Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act representation provisions and NCIP/DILG guidelines). **Not fully verified this session — treat as a gap.**
4. Sectoral representatives "in the manner provided by law" (largely dormant in practice; no enabling statute has operationalized their election — widely noted but not separately verified here).

### A5. Barangay Level and Relationship Upward

- **Punong Barangay** — chief executive of the barangay: **Sec. 389** (Chapter 2, Title I, Book III). ⚠️ Numbering consistent with ChanRobles Book III layout (Sec. 387, verified verbatim in fetch: "There shall be in each barangay a punong barangay, seven (7) sangguniang barangay members, the sangguniang kabataan chairman, a barangay secretary, and a barangay treasurer."); the "389 = Chief Executive: Punong Barangay" heading follows immediately in the same chapter but fell just beyond the readable portion this session. Medium-high confidence; verify before publication.
- **Sangguniang Barangay** — **Sec. 397**: composed of the punong barangay (presiding officer), seven (7) regular members, and the Sangguniang Kabataan chairman; in barangays with IP populations, an IPMR serves as an additional ex-officio member.
  - Verified (composition substance): Wikipedia "Sangguniang Barangay": seven regular members elected at large; SK chairperson as ex-officio eighth member; IPMR as ex-officio ninth member where applicable, citing COMELEC and a DILG-hosted Book III PDF (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sangguniang_Barangay, retrieved 2026-08-26). Exact subsection letter of Sec. 397 not pinned this session.
- **Relationship upward — Liga ng mga Barangay (ABC):**
  - All punong barangays compose the municipal Liga chapter (ABC); the chapter's president sits **ex officio** on the Sangguniang Bayan (via Sec. 446(a), above). The same pattern repeats upward (city/provincial chapter presidents sit on city/provincial sanggunians).
  - Liga legal basis: **Secs. 491–492** (creation/organization of liga chapters and federations); **Sec. 495** (powers, functions, duties of the Liga — verified: Wikipedia "Liga ng mga Barangay" quotes Sec. 495 verbatim, citing the DILG-hosted "Local Government Code, Book 3" PDF; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liga_ng_mga_Barangay, retrieved 2026-08-26). ⚠️ Exact split between Secs. 491/492 not independently verified this session.

### B2. Documented conflicts (do NOT carry forward silently)

1. **English Wikipedia infobox is unreliable for Maddela officials.** Both the Nov 1, 2024 revision (oldid 1254678539, via Wayback http://web.archive.org/web/20241104000500/https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maddela) and the current revision (retrieved 2026-08-26, edited Apr 2, 2026) list **Mayor: Florante T. Ruiz / Vice Mayor: Rimel C. Tolentino**. This contradicts: (a) the official municipal roster (Tolentino mayor, Ramos VM, Oct 2022); (b) PNA/Inquirer (Ruiz died Sept 2020); (c) Rappler 2025 results (Tolentino the mayoral winner in 2025). The Ruiz/Tolentino pairing matches the pre-2020 lineup and appears stale/wrong. **Do not use Wikipedia for Maddela officeholders.** Its "2019–2022" table (Mayor "Tolentino", VM "Joel Badogen", cited to a COMELEC 2019 PDF) is likewise inconsistent with news reports that Ruiz was the elected mayor who resigned/died in Sept 2020 while Tolentino was vice mayor — use only with corroboration.
2. **Identity of "Lorene Ace A. Ruiz" vs "Ruiz Ace Pong":** Rappler's 2025 results list councilor candidate "RUIZ ACE PONG (NPC)" ranked 4th; the 2022–2025 roster lists SB member "LORENE ACE A. RUIZ". Likely the same person (nickname "Ace Pong"), but **not confirmed** — do not merge names in publication without verification.
3. **Identity of vice-mayoral winner "SALVADOR JUNJUN" (2025):** likely Orlando L. Salvador, Jr. (2022–2025 SB member), but **not confirmed** — inference only.
