Key provisions captured verbatim from the retrieval: Sec. 6 requires all government agencies including LGUs to set up their most current and updated service standards as a Citizen's Charter (information billboards at office entrances/conspicuous places, on their websites, and published materials in English/Filipino/local dialect) detailing: (a) checklist of requirements per application type; (b) procedure to obtain a service; (c) person/s responsible per step; (d) maximum processing time; (e) documents to be presented; (f) amount of fees, if necessary; (g) procedure for filing complaints. Sec. 9(b)(1) caps processing at three (3) working days for simple transactions and seven (7) working days for complex transactions; twenty (20) working days maximum for applications involving danger to public health/safety/morals/policy or highly technical applications; extension only once for the same number of days; forty-five (45) working days (+20 extendible) where Sangguniang Bayan approval is required. These are statutory ceilings, not Maddela commitments.

- Candidate data: Republic Act No. 11032, "Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act of 2018" (amending RA 9485, Anti-Red Tape Act of 2007). Approved May 28, 2018.
